    1. Submit

      Upload a PDF, write notes or share a link, and choose the syllabus topic it covers.

    2. Review

      A moderator checks it for accuracy and relevance. You can follow its status in your account.

    3. Publish

      Once approved it appears in the library, credited to you, for every IB student to use.
